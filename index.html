Science Quiz

1. If a block of wood has a mass of 30 grams and a volume of 60 cubic centimeters what is its density, and will it float in pure water?

2. Which region of the Hertzsprung-Russell (H-R) diagram contains stars that are relatively cool in surface temperature (appearing red or orange) yet have high luminosity due to their massive physical size?

3. The San Andreas Fault in California is a classic example of which type of plate boundary where lithospheric plates slide past one another horizontally without creating or destroying crust?

4. Due to the Coriolis effect in the Northern Hemisphere, a free-moving wind or ocean current traveling from the equator toward the North Pole will be deflected in which direction?

5. Why is the number of trophic levels in an ecological food chain or energy pyramid typically limited to four or five?

3. On a standard Mercator projection map, which of the following spatial distortions occurs most severely?

4. The Danube River, Europe's second-longest river, flows through or borders 10 different countries before emptying into which body of water?

5. Which massive mountain range forms the traditional, conventional geographic boundary that divides the continents of Europe and Asia?
